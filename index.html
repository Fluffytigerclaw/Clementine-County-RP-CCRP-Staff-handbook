Staff conduct

Staff set the tone for the whole server. These apply to every rank, every time.

Be fair

  • Same rule, same punishment, whoever the player is.
  • Never punish friends differently, or enemies harder.

Be evidence-first

  • No evidence, no punishment.
  • Screenshot or clip every incident before acting.

Stay calm

  • Never argue in public. Move heated chats to a ticket or DM.
  • Rude players do not earn rude staff.

No abuse of power

  • No commands for fun, favours or revenge.
  • Staff follow the server rules too.

Stay private

  • Keep staff chats, reports and appeals inside staff channels.
  • Never leak who reported someone.

Do

  • Explain the rule in one calm sentence
  • Log every action in detail
  • Thank players who follow instructions

Do not

  • Punish when you are angry
  • Use staff powers for fun
  • Share staff chats or evidence publicly

Ask when unsure

  • Not sure it is a violation? Ask a senior mod first.
  • Asking is never a weakness.

Punishment guide

Follow the ladder from left to right: 1st offense to 5th. Check the player's logged history first, then pick the next step. For most rules the first time is a warning. Severe rules start with a ban.

minor

Small mistakes that mostly affect the roleplay. Warn, explain and move on.

moderate

Clear rule breaks that affect other players. Follow the ladder.

major

Serious harm to players or the server. Stricter steps. Get senior approval for long bans.

severe

Safety, cheating or Roblox TOS. Start with a ban and tell management.

Quick punishment lookup

Pick the rule and how many times the player has already been logged for it. You get the next step on the ladder.

Reading the ladder

Warnings count as offenses and must be logged, even verbal ones for small or accidental mistakes. Offenses count within the player's logged history. Staff can go up a step for aggravating factors (repeat behavior, targeting, clear intent) or down a step for a genuine first mistake, but must write the reason in the log. Bans of 7 days or more, and all permanent bans, need senior mod approval.

Handling a report

Use this every time. It keeps decisions consistent and easy to appeal.

Gather evidence

Get a clip or screenshot. Check the in-game logs and ask the reporter for the time, players involved and location.

Check the history

Look up the player's previous logs so you know which step of the ladder they are on.

Join and observe

If the incident is live, join the session, watch quietly and avoid jumping to conclusions.

Give the correct punishment

Use the guide. Tell the player the rule broken in one clear sentence, and stay polite.

Log it

Write a detailed log: who, which rule and offense number, where, how you found it (a !mod call or patrol), what you saw step by step, your evidence and what you did. No log means it did not happen.

Escalate if needed

Unsure, out of your rank, or the player disputes it? Pass it to a senior mod or admin and do not argue.

Writing a detailed log

A log should let a senior mod who was not there picture exactly what happened. Say how you got involved, what you saw, and what you did about it. One vague line is not enough.

Every log needs

  • The player's Roblox username
  • The rule broken and which offense it is
  • Where it happened and about what time
  • How you found it: a !mod call or your own patrol
  • What you saw and heard, in order
  • Your evidence (clip or screenshot link)
  • The punishment you gave
Good log: mod call

“I came to this mod call after PlayerA used !mod at the hospital. When I arrived, PlayerA told me PlayerB shot them with no roleplay first. I asked PlayerB for their side and they said they just wanted to. I checked the clip and confirmed no interaction happened before the shot. This is PlayerB's first RDM, so I gave a warning and explained the rule. Clip: [link].”

Good log: patrol

“I was driving on Highway 55 and saw PlayerC going 125 mph past a police car and weaving through traffic with no roleplay reason. I followed for about 30 seconds and it did not stop. I recorded a clip and sent them a :pm about the rule. This is Fail Driving, first offense, so I gave a warning. Clip: [link].”

Bad log

“Warned him for RDM.”

Who? Where? How did you find out? What did you actually see? Nobody can check or appeal a log like this.

Mod toolkit

        

          Extra help for tricky moments. Use it when you are unsure whether something is really a violation,
          how serious it is, what evidence to collect, or what to say.
        

        
Is it really a violation? Ask these six questions

        

          

            
Is there a written rule?

            
If you cannot point to a rule in this handbook, do not punish. Ask a senior mod.

          

          

            
Do I have evidence?

            
A clip, screenshot or in-game log. No evidence means a friendly reminder at most.

          

          

            
Was it on purpose?

            
Accidents, lag and honest mistakes usually get a warning, not a ban.

          

          

            
What was the context?

            

              Was there an approved priority, a roleplay setup or a reason before the action? Look at the
              whole scene.
            

          

          

            
What is their history?

            
Check logs so you know which step of the ladder they are on.

          

          

            
Is this the lowest fair step?

            
Pick the step that fixes the behavior without being harsher than needed.

          

        

        
Moving a punishment up or down

        

          

            Aggravating (go up a step)
            
    
              
  • Repeated right after a warning
    • 
              
  • Targeting one player
    • 
              
  • Lying or hiding evidence
    • 
              
  • Ignoring staff instructions
    • 
              
  • Doing it to many players
    • 
            

          

          

            Mitigating (go down a step)
            
    
              
  • Honest first mistake
    • 
              
  • New player
    • 
              
  • Lag or a real disconnect
    • 
              
  • Apologized and fixed it
    • 
              
  • Followed staff instructions at once
    • 
            

          

          

            
Always log your reason

            
If you move a step up or down, write why in the log. Senior mods can then check it.

          

        

        
What evidence to collect

        

          

            

              Rule
              Best evidence
            

            

              RDM, VDM, Mass RDM
              :killlogs plus a clip that shows there was no roleplay first.
            

            

              GTA / Reckless Driving
              A clip with the speedometer, the location and no police chasing.
            

            

              Combat logging / LTAP
              The time they left, who they were with, and when they rejoined.
            

            

              Chat rules, disrespect, filter bypass
              A screenshot with usernames and time. Never cut out the context.
            

            

              Exploiting / hacking
              A clip that shows the exploit, and the username.
            

            

              Staff evasion
              The mod call time and a clip of the player leaving.
            

            

              Priority violations
              Timer screenshot and the time of the crime.
            

          

        

        
Say this, not that

        

          

            

              Instead of
              Say
            

            

              "You are wrong."
              "I understand. The rule says [rule], and the clip shows [what happened]."
            

            

              "I am staff, so I decide."
              "Here is the rule and the evidence. You can appeal in [appeals channel]."
            

            

              "Stop crying."
              "I hear you. Let's move this to a ticket."
            

            

              "Whatever, you are banned."
              "You have been banned for [rule]. You can appeal after reading the rules."
            

          

        

        
Hard situations

        

          

            
An angry player

            

              Stay calm and use short sentences. State the rule once, then move it to a ticket or DM. Do not
              argue.
            

          

          

            
A friend broke a rule

            

              Treat them like anyone else and log it. If you cannot be fair, hand the case to another mod.
            

          

          

            
Several trolls at once

            

              Call other staff, collect names and clips, and deal with the worst offenders first. Use bans for
              severe cases.
            

          

          

            
A conflict with another staff member

            
Do not argue in public. Take it to an Administrator in a private chat with your evidence.

          

        

      

      

        
Priority requests

        

          A priority is a server-wide crime event, so only one can run at a time. Players ask for one with
          !mod. Use this page to decide quickly and fairly. These are common ER:LC standards, so edit them to
          match CCRP.
        

        

          

            
Read the !mod call

            
Find out who is asking, what they want to do and where.

          

          

            
Check for a timer

            

              If a priority or peace timer is already running above the minimap, ask them to wait until it
              ends.
            

          

          

            
Check the roleplay is allowed

            
Use the allowed and banned lists below. Banned roleplay is a hard no.

          

          

            
Check group size and place

            
Keep the group within the limit and make sure it is not in a safe zone or at a spawn.

          

          

            
Approve or deny

            
Tell the player clearly. If you deny, say why and what they can do instead.

          

          

            
Start the timer and announce it

            

              Use :prty seconds and an :h hint. The Hints tab builds both for you.
            

          

          

            
Watch, log, then end it

            

              Stay near the scene, log what happened, then start a peace timer with :pt when it
              ends.
            

          

        

        
Quick check: can I approve this?

        

          
Tick every statement that is true for the request.

          

          

        

        
Allowed with staff approval

        

          

            
Bank robbery

            
Needs an approved priority. Many servers want a group of 3 or more and a realistic plan.

          

          

            
Jewelry store robbery

            
Usually 2 or more players, ideally with a hostage.

          

          

            
Armed, gas station and store robberies

            
Allowed with a priority. Space them out so players are not robbing everything in a row.

          

          

            
Hostage and kidnapping scenes

            
Needs approval first. The hostage player must be willing to roleplay it.

          

          

            
Pursuits and planned shootouts

            
A priority stops other crimes from starting at the same time.

          

          

            
Other server-wide events

            
Anything every department would respond to needs a priority too.

          

        

        
Banned and restricted roleplays

        

          

            Always banned
            
    
              
  • Bomb roleplay and terrorism
    • 
              
  • Suicide and self-harm
    • 
              
  • Drug use and drunk roleplay
    • 
              
  • NSFW and sexual roleplay
    • 
              
  • Domestic abuse
    • 
              
  • Chemical weapons
    • 
              
  • Anything that breaks Roblox TOS
    • 
            

          

          

            Restricted, ask first
            
    
              
  • Hitman roleplay
    • 
              
  • Kidnapping EMS, fire or DOT staff
    • 
              
  • Checkpoints and border roleplay
    • 
              
  • Gangs and mafias (whitelisted only)
    • 
            

          

          

            
Punishing banned roleplay

            

              See "Banned Roleplays" and "Breaking Roblox TOS" in the Punishment Guide, and log it in detail.
            

          

        

        
Good reasons to deny

        

          
    
            
  • Another priority or a peace timer is active
    • 
            
  • The roleplay is banned or restricted and not approved
    • 
            
  • The group is too big or the request is a way around the group limit
    • 
            
  • They are in a safe zone or at a spawn
    • 
            
  • No realistic plan, like no roles, outfits or hostage
    • 
            
  • They already had a priority very recently
    • 
          

        

        
Reply templates

        

          

            Approved
            

              Your priority for [roleplay] is approved. The timer is starting now. Keep it realistic and
              follow staff instructions.
            

          

          

            Wait
            

              Another priority or peace timer is active right now. Please wait until it ends and ask again.
            

          

          

            Denied
            
That roleplay is not allowed in CCRP because [reason]. You can do [allowed option] instead.

          

        

      

      

        
:h commands

        

          Hints show a short message at the top of every player's screen. Use them to announce priorities,
          peacetime and rule reminders. Commands below come from the ER:LC private server command list.
        

        

          

            

              Command
              What it does
            

            

              :h text
              Server-wide hint at the top of every screen. Free to use.
            

            

              :m text
              Server-wide pop-up message. Some servers keep this for senior staff, so check first.
            

            

              :pm player text
              Private message to one player.
            

            

              :prty seconds
              Priority timer above the minimap. Needs the premium server pack. Seconds only.
            

            

              :pt seconds
              
                Peace timer above the minimap. Players cannot damage each other while it runs. Needs the
                premium pack.
              
            

            

              :killlogs
              List of killed players and who killed them. Great evidence for RDM and VDM.
            

          

        

        

          

            
Keep it short

            
Hints disappear fast. One clear sentence works best.

          

          

            
Use seconds

            
Timers only take seconds. 5 minutes is 300, 10 minutes is 600, 20 minutes is 1200.

          

          

            
Do not spam

            
Too many hints annoy players. Announce once, then let the scene play.

Player questions

        

          Players ask staff the same things again and again. Use these answers so every staff member says the
          same thing. Replies have [brackets] for things only CCRP knows, like channel names. Fill those in
          once in the list and they are ready to copy.
        

        

          

            
Be friendly

            
A short, kind answer keeps players happy and the server welcoming.

          

          

            
Never promise

            
Do not promise staff roles, promotions, unbans or perks. Only management can.

          

          

            
Point to the right place

            
Send players to the application, ticket or appeal channel instead of handling it in chat.

          

          

            
Keep staff business private

            
Do not share who is being considered, who reported someone, or staff chats.

          

        

        

          
        

        

      

      

        
Ban appeals

        

          Every player deserves a fair second look. A recommended process is below. Edit it to match how CCRP
          really handles appeals.
        

        

          

            
Read the appeal

            
Note the username, the ban reason and length, and why the player says it should be lifted.

          

          

            
Pull the original log and evidence

            

              A vague log or missing evidence counts in the player's favor. This is why detailed logs matter.
            

          

          

            
Check their history

            
Look at earlier offenses and earlier appeals so you know how serious the pattern is.

          

          

            
Decide

            

              Accept and unban, reduce the ban, or deny. Ask an Administrator or higher when unsure. Try not
              to review your own punishments.
            

          

          

            
Reply with a clear reason

            
Be short, polite and specific. Never argue or insult, even if the player does.

          

          

            
Log the outcome

            
Record who decided, what was decided and why.

          

        

        
What to look for

        

          

            
Strong appeals

            
    
              
  • Honest about what happened
    • 
              
  • Shows they understand the rule
    • 
              
  • Respectful and clear
    • 
            

          

          

            
Weak appeals

            
    
              
  • Blames staff or other players
    • 
              
  • Denies it with no explanation
    • 
              
  • Rude, spammy or copy-pasted
    • 
            

          

          

            
Usually not appealable

            

              Exploiting, hate speech, ban evasion, raiding and Roblox TOS breaks. Management decides these
              case by case.
            

          

        

        
Reply templates

        

          

            Accepted
            

              Hello [name], thanks for your appeal. We checked the log and evidence and have lifted your ban.
              Please read the rules again, as a repeat offense may lead to a longer ban.
            

          

          

            Reduced
            

              Hello [name], thanks for your appeal. After reviewing the log, we have reduced your ban to
              [length] because [reason]. Please follow the rules when you return.
            

          

          

            Denied
            

              Hello [name], thank you for your appeal. After reviewing the log and evidence, the ban stays
              because [reason]. You may appeal again after [time].

R/A checklist

          

            Tick items off as you complete them. Progress is saved on this device. Edit the list to match

Responding to a !mod call

        

          

            
Before you join

            

              Read the call and note the players and location. Tell staff chat you are taking it so two mods
              do not join the same call.
            

          

          

            
When you arrive

            

              Stay calm and hear both sides. Do not pick a side yet. Note the time and start recording if you
              can.
            

          

          

            
While handling it

            

              Check the evidence, find the rule, and use the lookup for the next step. Ask a senior mod if you
              are unsure.
            

          

          

            
After

            

              Give the punishment, explain the rule in one sentence, and write a detailed log that starts "I
              came to this mod call...".

In-game commands

        

          Common ERLC moderation commands. Which ones you can use depends on your in-game rank, so check
          :cmds in game for your list.
        

        

          

            

              Command
              What it does
            

            

              :kick user reason
              Removes a player from the server. Use for warnings that need a reset.
            

            

              :ban user
              Bans a player from the private server. Log the reason and length.
            

            

              :unban user
              Lifts a ban after an approved appeal.
            

            

              :pm user message
              Private message a player. Good for quiet warnings.
            

            

              :m message
              Server-wide message. Use sparingly.
            

            

              :h message
              Hint at the top of every player's screen.
            

            

              :mod user / :unmod user
              Gives or removes in-game moderator. Management only.
            

            

              :log / :logs
              View recent command logs and actions.
            

          

        

      


      

        
Staff ranks

        
What each rank handles. Edit the names in this file to match your server.

        

          

            
Trial Moderator

            

              Not a full moderator yet. Must complete an R/A (ride-along) with an Administrator or higher
              before being promoted. Log everything you do and ask when unsure.
            

          

          

            
Moderator

            
Handles reports, warns, kicks and short bans. Mentors trial mods.

          

          

            
Senior Moderator

            
Approves long bans, reviews logs, and settles disputes between players and staff.

          

          

            
Administrator

            

              Runs R/As for trial moderators, handles appeals and staff issues, and keeps rules and handbook
              up to date.
            

          

          

            
Management

            

              Can run R/As too. Final say on bans, staff promotions and demotions, and the direction of CCRP.
            

          

        

      


      

        
FAQ

        
Quick answers for the situations that come up the most.

        

          What is an R/A?
          

            

              An R/A is a ride-along. Trial moderators are not full mods yet, so they complete an R/A with an
              Administrator or higher first. Watch how they handle reports, ask questions, and write your logs
              as if you were on your own.
            

          

        

        

          A player says I am wrong. What now?
          

            

              Stay calm, state the rule and point them to the appeal process. Do not argue or debate in chat.
            

          

        

        

          I am not sure which rule applies.
          

            
Do not guess. Ask a senior mod in staff chat and wait for a reply before acting.

          

        

        

          Can I punish someone without evidence?
          

            
No. Without evidence you can only warn verbally. Ban decisions always need proof.

          

        

        

          How detailed should my log be?
          

            

              Detailed enough that a senior mod could picture it. Say how you got involved (a !mod call or
              your patrol), where you were, what you saw step by step, your evidence and what you did. See the
              examples on the Procedure tab.
            

          

        

        

          A player says they did not know the rule.
          

            

              Point them to the rules and give the normal step. A first-time warning already covers honest
              mistakes. Do not skip logging it.
            

          

        

        

          Someone sent me a clip. Can I punish from it?
          

            

              Only if the clip clearly shows the whole incident and you can tell who is who. If it is cut or
              unclear, ask for more evidence or review the in-game logs first.
            

          

        

        

          Two players both broke rules in one incident.
          

            

              Punish each one separately using their own history, and write a log for each. Do not let one
              player's rule break excuse the other's.
            

          

        

        

          The player leaves in the middle of the report.
          

            

              Log what you saw, keep the evidence, and note that they left. Leaving to avoid punishment can
              count as combat logging or LTAP.
            

          

        

        

          A staff member broke a rule.
          

            
Report it privately to an administrator with evidence. Never call staff out in public.

          

        

        

          A player is using an exploit or hacks.
          

            
Collect evidence, ban immediately if you have the rank, and tell a senior mod right after.
